India Tender Portal

An open-source project exploring ways to simplify government tender discovery and management in India.

The Problem

Government tender information is distributed across multiple procurement portals. Contractors and small businesses often spend considerable time finding relevant opportunities, understanding eligibility requirements, and tracking submission deadlines.

The Proposed Solution

India Tender Portal aims to bring essential tender information and management tools into one accessible platform.

Planned Features

* Tender discovery and search
* Tender summaries and eligibility analysis
* Submission deadline tracking
* Document organization
* Notifications for relevant opportunities
* AI-assisted analysis of tender documents

Project Status

Early-stage project. Development and feature planning are in progress.

Vision

To make government tender information easier to navigate, particularly for small contractors and businesses.

Contributions

Contributions, suggestions, and feedback will be welcome as the project develops.
